# Full event agreements and generated name signatures

This extends the booking contract flow with the owner's updated Photo Booth and Live Photo Magnet Service Agreement (20 sections). The source version and original DOCX SHA-256 are recorded with the document. Private business contact details are configured in the authenticated app, not checked into this public repository.

## Activation

Deploy the booking app build, then open **Contracts** in the Shopify app. Complete the provider legal name, mailing address, phone, notices email, and the actual backup deletion policy. The latter is a business decision and is deliberately not invented. Select the existing add-on that adds one live-service hour per unit, if offered. Confirm USD and the approved 30% retainer in booking settings. Enable the full agreement for new bookings.

The feature is disabled until configured. Existing bookings keep their previous agreement. No customer signatures, payments, emails, production configuration or existing records are generated by activating the code.

## Customer flow

1. The customer chooses their date, collection and extras and supplies the existing booking information plus a mailing address. Optional fields include event name, event-day contact and entity signer/title.
2. Generate my contract saves the booking and a snapshot of the business terms and booking data. The private booking page displays all 20 completed sections with section links and Download PDF.
3. The customer reviews the contract, edits the name if needed, and chooses Classic or Handwritten style. Both are visual renderings of a typed name, not handwriting reproduction or identity verification.
4. Consent starts unchecked. Only Apply my signature (or Apply my signature & accept quote) saves the signature. The receipt includes the name, booking email, chosen style, server timestamp, consent, revision and exact document hash.
5. The baseline requires both parties to sign. An authenticated owner reviews the same copy and explicitly countersigns with their name, title and consent. This rechecks availability for a held booking.
6. Only after both signatures are saved can the customer choose 30% prepayment or the full amount through Shopify Checkout. Payment verification, not signing, confirms receipt and the booking.

The private page supplies the completed copy to both parties through their existing access. No signed-PDF email attachment or third-party electronic-signature service is introduced.

## Amendments and records

Material changes produce a new revision and clear current signatures. Earlier signed copies retain both receipts in history. Pending invoices must be voided before changing signed contents. Changes to global contract settings only affect new bookings unless an owner explicitly selects the latest settings while editing a booking.

The rendered document is retained while its server-side booking input fingerprint remains unchanged, so a code/template deployment alone does not silently replace an already reviewed copy. Stored document hashes are independently checked. Customer input cannot set provider identity, pricing, negotiated terms or owner-only logistics.

Missing essential fields and unsupported payment terms block signing. Later planning details such as station location or final shipping address are explicitly described as to be confirmed. Owner agreement editing is available on the booking details screen.

## Validation

72 automated tests cover legacy behavior, full agreement generation, placeholder prevention, explicit consent and appearance, stale hashes, owner authorization, countersign idempotence, amendment history, cross-shop denial, pending invoice protection and 30/70 payment separation. TypeScript and the production build are also checked.

The sample preview uses invented contact details and no live booking, payment, email or signature. The Shopify validator accepts the new settings components; standalone checks of existing route files cannot resolve their server-only Node/app imports, while the project's full typecheck passes.

PDF export generates a real application/pdf file in the browser with bundled fonts. It does not open a blank HTML window or depend on a print dialog. A retained Save PDF file link and Open PDF to print link provide a fallback after generation. Signed copies include both stored receipts and the selected name appearance; unsigned copies are clearly marked. PDF code/fonts load only when requested, and no contract content is sent to an external PDF service.
